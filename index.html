    Learning from mistakes

    When a car crashes, stalls or has a close call, the game rewinds to 0.9, 0.5 and 0.2 seconds before it and replays each moment with 15 different steering and braking choices, on the same road with the same pedestrians and traffic. Whichever choice gets through safely and keeps making progress becomes the lesson. Every car still driving trains its brain on it straight away, and each new generation studies the saved lessons before it sets off. Evolution then keeps the brains that drive best.

    Training

    More lines help cars spot small obstacles and gaps but make the simulation a little slower. Changing this adapts every brain, so the cars keep what they learned, and restarts the current generation.
